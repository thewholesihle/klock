What it does

Everything a clock needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

16 dials

Hand-tuned colours that fill the whole screen, each with a matching night look.

Three movements

  • Quartz ticks once a second.
  • Mechanical swings and settles.
  • Smooth glides without a break.

Night lume

Hands and indices glow in one of 14 colours after dark.

OLED-safe

The whole face drifts by a few pixels so nothing burns in.

Every monitor

One clock per display, in sync. Pauses when the display sleeps and slows on battery.

10 languages

Including right-to-left. No account, no telemetry.

Three steps. No account.

  1. 1

    Install

    Run the installer. It places the screensaver where Windows looks for it.

  2. 2

    Pick your look

    Open Settings from the Screen Saver dialog. Choose a dial, a movement and a typeface.

  3. 3

    Leave it on

    Klock keeps exact time, and pauses when the display is off.
